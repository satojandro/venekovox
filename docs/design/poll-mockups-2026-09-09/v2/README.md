## Narrative and design decisions

Reviewed apps/front-end/src/pages/Landing.tsx and styles/index.css. Preserve the existing narrative from being silenced or drowned out, through participation, to inspecting evidence. Replace newspaper-like serif headlines with confident sans-serif; retain forest/lime heritage with a porcelain light theme.

Welcome skepticism without defining members by conspiratorial beliefs or political affiliation. Proposed umbrella line: “You don’t have to agree to belong here.” The promise is an equal opportunity to participate and evidence to inspect, not validation of any belief. Do not promise absolute privacy, zero bots or permanent protection; those claims in the earlier landing copy are not adopted.

Polls use ballot previews, explicit eligibility and participation CTAs. Final implementation should consider all six options or a neutral “6 options” summary rather than permanently privileging the first two candidates in preview cards.

Logged-in status and poll eligibility remain separate. Verification reuse is session-scoped; grants remain poll-scoped. The comparison uses two distinct polls, France-only and EU-wide, which includes France. A true France-versus-rest-of-EU view needs authenticated country data and the Stage 2 release gate. Self-selected samples are not representative electorate estimates.

Mission copy is proposed, not an invented factual founding story. An actual origin timeline requires owner-provided history.

## Visual inspection and implementation notes

- Show eligibility requirements with neutral icons before verification; reserve completion checkmarks for confirmed predicates.
- Replace generated decorative “A more representative tomorrow” wording with “A more open tomorrow”; no representativeness claim.
- Make coordinator visibility and issuer responsibilities explicit in the deeper trust disclosure.
- Keep theme selection persistent and respect system preference; dark is the lead presentation, not a forced preference for everyone.

No runtime changes, new product gates, live accounts, deployments or results. Changes remain uncommitted.
